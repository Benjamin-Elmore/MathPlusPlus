A car covers $d \coloneqq  120$ in $t \coloneqq  1.5$, so its speed is

\begin{equation*}
  v \coloneqq  \frac{d}{t}
\end{equation*}
\begin{equation*}
  v = 80
\end{equation*}
Doubling the time halves the speed:

\begin{equation*}
  \frac{d}{2 t} = 40
\end{equation*}
