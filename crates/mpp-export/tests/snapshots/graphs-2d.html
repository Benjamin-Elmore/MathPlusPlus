A Figure: line draws a graph. List what to draw, separated by commas, and give a range for the variable with x \in [a, b]. Text after the first . is the caption.

Asymptotes and jumps

The curve is broken at an asymptote instead of joined by a vertical line.

At a jump, an open dot marks the end that is not included and a closed dot the end that is.
